Listing rules

What you can list

Product websites and X (Twitter) @handles — SaaS, apps, tools, startups, agencies, services. The listing must be a real, working product or business page.

What you cannot list

No chat or invite links (WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord, etc.). No sexual or NSFW content. No affiliate, referral or tracking links — tracking parameters are stripped. No link shorteners. No gambling, crypto-doubling, or get-rich-quick schemes. Listings that break these rules are removed without notice.

How ranking works

Rank is purely your bid amount, highest first. Ties go to the earlier listing. To take a rank, bid ₹50 more than the listing currently holding it. Resubmitting the same URL or handle tops up your existing listing instead of creating a duplicate.

Launch phase & payments

During the launch phase, claiming a rank is free and your bid amount reserves your rank. When paid bidding opens, holding a rank requires paying your bid; you will be notified by email before that happens. Once paid bidding is live, payments are final and non-refundable, and topping up charges only the difference.

Moderation

OutBid is moderated by BrandiQue Web Solutions. We may edit categories, hide or remove listings that break these rules or misrepresent the product. Questions: reach us via brandique.in.
