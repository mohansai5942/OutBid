Privacy Policy

What we collect

When you claim a rank, we store your product's public details (name, tagline, URL, logo) and the email address you provide for rank alerts and listing updates. We count clicks on listings to show click statistics. We do not collect or store payment card details — checkout is handled entirely by our payment partner.

How we use it

Listing details are displayed publicly on the board — that is the point of the service. Your email is used only for service messages about your listing (rank changes, launch-phase updates, payment notices) and, if you are a product owner, occasional information about BrandiQue services relevant to founders. We do not sell your data.

Public metadata

When you enter a product URL, we read its publicly available metadata (page title, description, logo) to build your listing preview. We only fetch what the site itself publishes openly.

Your choices & contact

Ask us to update or delete your listing and associated email at any time via brandique.in and we will action it promptly. Deleting a listing removes it from the board; completed payments remain non-refundable per the Terms.
