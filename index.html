FAQ

What can I list?

Product websites and X (Twitter) @handles — SaaS, apps, tools, startups, agencies. No chat/invite links, no NSFW, no affiliate or tracking links.

How does ranking work exactly?

Rank is purely your bid amount, highest first. Ties go to the earlier listing. To take a rank, bid ₹50 more than the listing currently holding it.

Is it really free right now?

Yes — during the launch phase, claims cost nothing and your bid amount reserves your rank. When paid bidding opens, you'll only pay the difference to keep your spot.

Can I raise my rank later?

Yes. Resubmit the same URL or handle with a higher bid — you only pay the difference.
