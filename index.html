    FAQ

    What is OutBid?

    A public leaderboard for Indian products and startups where rank is bought, not voted on. No upvotes, no hunter, no review queue.

    How does ranking work exactly?

    All-time rank is everything you have ever paid for that listing — it never expires. Today rank is what you spent since midnight UTC. Equal amounts stay in the order they were placed: the older listing keeps the higher rank.

    Is it really free right now?

    Yes — during the launch phase, claims cost nothing and your bid amount reserves your rank. When paid bidding opens, you'll be notified by email before anything is charged.

    Can I raise my rank later?

    Yes. Enter the same URL or @handle again with a higher amount — it tops up your existing listing instead of creating a duplicate.

    Are payments refundable?

    Once paid bidding opens, a completed payment is what claims the rank and payments are not refundable.
